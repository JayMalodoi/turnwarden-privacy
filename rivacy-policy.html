Privacy Policy for Turn Warden

Last updated: May 5, 2026

Turn Warden is a tabletop role-playing game turn tracker and display companion app. This privacy policy explains how the app handles information on your device.

Information We Collect

Turn Warden does not collect, sell, share, or transmit personal data to the developer or to third-party services.

Information Stored on Your Device

The app may store settings and game preparation data locally on your device, such as:

  • Display device IP address
  • Standalone mode preference
  • Player and monster names
  • Images selected by the user for local use in the app
  • Timer and display preferences

This information stays on your device unless you choose to remove the app data or uninstall the app.

Network Access

Turn Warden uses network access to communicate with a display device on your local network when you use controller/display mode. This communication is used only for app features such as connection checks, timer state, and turn tracker display updates.

Photos and Images

If you choose an image for a player or monster, Android grants Turn Warden access to that selected image. The app uses the image only inside Turn Warden and does not upload it to the developer or to third-party services.

Third-Party Services

Turn Warden does not use analytics, advertising SDKs, account systems, or third-party tracking services.

Children's Privacy

Turn Warden is not designed to collect personal information from children. Because the app does not collect personal data, no children's personal data is knowingly collected.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the app changes. The updated version will be published at this same location.

Contact

For privacy questions about Turn Warden, please use the developer contact information available on the app's Google Play listing.
